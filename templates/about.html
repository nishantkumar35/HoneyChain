About HoneyChain

A blockchain-based honey traceability and smart beekeeping management system.

🐝

Our Mission

Honey is one of the most adulterated foods in the world. Consumers rarely know where their honey was harvested, which floral sources the bees visited, or if the product has been diluted with high-fructose corn syrup.

HoneyChain empowers honest beekeepers to register every harvest and record its cryptographic SHA-256 fingerprint on an immutable blockchain. With a simple QR scan on any smartphone, consumers can verify origin, extraction details, and authenticity in seconds.

🏡

Smart Beekeeping

Track apiary locations, colonies, subspecies, and colony health across multiple seasons in one clean interface.

  • ✓ Colony health tracking
  • ✓ Apiary geolocation recording
  • ✓ Harvest yield analytics
🔗

Blockchain Integrity

Deterministic SHA-256 batch hashes committed to a Solidity smart contract deployed on an Ethereum Hardhat network.

  • ✓ Immutable on-chain hashes
  • ✓ Automated tamper detection
  • ✓ Dynamic QR code verification
