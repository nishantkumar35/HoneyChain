How it works

From hive to jar, made simple

Three easy steps for beekeepers. One clear answer for customers.

Step 1

Record your apiary

Add each apiary and its colonies once. HoneyChain keeps your hives organised in one calm place.

Step 2

Create a honey batch

Log the harvest date, quantity and source. We store a secure fingerprint of the record on the blockchain.

Step 3

Share the QR code

Print the QR on your jar. Customers scan it and see the journey and whether it matches the blockchain.

Why customers trust it

Every jar carries a proof, not just a promise.

Each batch record is fingerprinted and stored on the blockchain. If anyone changes the record, the fingerprint stops matching and customers can see it.
